Pattern recognition is the throughline in my work. Great branding, great design and great content start when I can take disparate elements and bring them together in one cohesive story. Great stories are built by unearthing the unexpected patterns that, when made visible, feel inevitable. I do that pattern-finding work across language, ideas and visuals because the three together make for the stories that connect with people's identities.

0-1 B2B SaaS Brand identity

Basejoy Identity

Built Basejoy's identity from scratch: mark, voice and a design system down to a 15-icon set.

Basejoy was built on a promise to open up the black box that databases usually are. The mark reflects that opening, and the rest of the system carries it through with clear structure, a plainspoken voice, and color and type documented down to hex codes and weight ramps. The hardest part was the icon set: fifteen marks for abstract concepts like observability, lineage and threshold, each legible enough that someone outside engineering can still read it.

Growth stage B2B edtech Product branding

Inkwell Branding

Built Inkwell's identity from scratch, around making a new, complicated approach to curriculum feel accessible and like the next right step.

Inkwell was a new kind of product for the curriculum market: two core curricula, ELA and social studies, combined into one 70-minute instructional block. The idea is a two-for-one at its core, so the name and identity had to reflect both content areas. The assets around it then had to simplify the objections, pain points and questions that a new approach raises. As a second product, Inkwell also dovetailed with the creation of our initial brand architecture system for the inquirED parent brand and its sub-brands.

Established B2B services Icon design

Values Icons

Mapped five company values onto five tools from a geotechnical firm's own field.

In a pitch to Sanborn Head, a geotechnical engineering firm, I proposed icons for five internal values: integrity, financial strength, servant leadership, collegiality and employee engagement. Instead of pairing each value with a generic icon, I mapped it to a tool from their own field: a compass for integrity, an auger for financial strength, since soil layers reveal generational history.

About me

I'm a brand designer based in Seattle. I figure out what a company promises that's unique to them and matters to their customer. Then I direct, design and write everything that goes out into the world to make that case.

Before brand, I ran strategy and communications for education programs, so I write like a writer, I ship like a program manager, and I care whether a brand system gets used.

Outside of work I row, take photos of the tiles and typography in the wild, read genre fiction, eat a lot of ice cream, and try to be near a river or a lake whenever possible. A Saturday afternoon at my art table, drawing or painting, is my favorite kind of day.

I wanted to be a writer growing up, until I took my first college creative writing class and hated everyone in it. I'm terrible at bringing a gift for the host but I always do the dishes. It will take me 3 to 5 business days to answer your text even if you're my sister, but I will absolutely show up to water your garden while you're out of town. None of it resolves, but I find I like it better that way.
